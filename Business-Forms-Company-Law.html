1 · Three Business Forms 三種生意形式

Section 01

🟢 Sole Trader

Enters business on his own account — the ‘one-man band’.

  • Who acts: The individual = the business, not legally separated
  • Control: Complete personal control
  • Liability: Personal, unlimited
  • Continuity: Ends / disrupted by death or incapacity

🔵 中文解說：獨資 = 你就係間公司

一個人做晒老闆 + 員工。你同間公司係同一人，法律上冇分開。

即係：賺晒係你嘅，蝕晒都係你孭。間公司欠債 = 你個人欠債，債主可以直接追你個人財產。

🟢 Partnership

The relation which subsists between persons carrying on a business in common with a view to profit. — s.3 Partnership Ordinance

  • Who acts: Partners carry on business together
  • Control: Shared, under agreement
  • Liability: Joint / joint & several
  • Continuity: May terminate on death / bankruptcy of a partner

🔵 中文解說：合夥 = 夾份做生意為咗賺錢

幾個人一齊做生意，目的係賺錢。重點字：in common（共同）+ with a view to profit（為咗賺錢），考試要寫晒。

即係：一個拍檔簽咗約，可能成班都要孭。一人出事，成個合夥可能散。

🟢 Company

A company formed and registered under the Companies Ordinance. — s.2 CO

  • Who acts: Company is distinct from shareholders, directors, staff
  • Control: Board / constitution
  • Liability: Members’ liability limited
  • Continuity: Perpetual succession

🔵 中文解說：公司 = 獨立法律人

公司係一個「法律上嘅人」，同股東、董事唔同人。公司欠債唔等於股東欠債。

即係：公司執咗，股東最多輸晒買股票嘅錢，唔使攞自己層樓去還（除非有擔保）。公司唔會因為一個人死就自動消失。

🟡 點揀？考試三句：要控制 + 簡單 → Sole trader；要夾技能 / 資金又唔想搞公司手續 → Partnership；要有限責任 + 永久延續 + 容易融資 → Company。

2 · Sole Trader 獨資經營

Section 02

🟢 Advantages 優點

  • Take all profits
  • Absolute control over company policy
  • Flexibility: minimal legal requirements; no need to satisfy accounting provisions
  • Easy to transfer business

🔵 中文解說

自己話晒事，賺幾多袋幾多，唔使交公司帳目、唔使開會，轉讓手續都相對簡單。

🟢 Disadvantages 缺點

  • Personally liable for all debts; danger of getting bankrupt
  • Means of raising finance is limited
  • Death or incapacity will affect operation
  • Difficult to transfer business

🔵 中文解說

最大風險係無限責任：生意欠債 = 你個人破產風險。借錢難，因為銀行只睇你個人信用。你病咗 / 過身，生意即刻停。

🟡 原筆記矛盾點，考試點答？前面話「Easy to transfer」，後面話「Difficult to transfer」。答法：小生意轉手手續上可以好隨意，但因為冇獨立法人、冇股份可以轉，買家要重新建立信譽、合約、牌照，所以實際轉讓唔容易。寫埋呢句就攞到分。

🟢 Bankruptcy Pathway 破產程序

  1. Cannot satisfy all debts → creditors can apply for bankruptcy of sole proprietor
  2. Bankruptcy order issued → trustee appointed to take over bankrupt’s assets
  3. Trustee realises assets and distributes to creditors

🔵 中文解說

還唔到錢 → 債主申請你破產 → 法庭頒破產令 → 受託人接管你啲資產 → 變賣分畀債主。

🟢 Automatic Discharge 自動解除破產

After 4 years if: (1) No objection from creditors / trustee, (2) Not previously adjudged bankrupt, (3) Complies with Bankruptcy Ordinance.
Extended to 5 years if previously adjudged bankrupt.

🔵 中文解說

4年後自動「洗底」要同時滿足3個條件。之前破產過 → 變5年。考試最鍾意考 4 vs 5。

🔵 Alternative — 向法庭 / 債主提出還款方案：避免破產污名 / 唔受《破產條例》綁住 / 好大機會保得住份工 / 債主有望收返多啲。考試寫：唔係唔還，係換個方法還。

3 · Partnership 合夥

Section 03

🟢 Default Rules 冇協議點算？

Partnership Ordinance applies in absence of agreement. E.g. profits and losses shared equally. All assets contributed shall be property of all partners. A minor can be a partner but is not jointly liable.

🔵 中文解說

冇寫合夥協議，就跟《合夥條例》預設規則：賺蝕對分、大家夾出嚟嘅資產係共同擁有。未成年人都可以做合夥人，但唔使承擔連帶責任。

🟡 考試重心 — Agency 代理：每個合夥人都是公司嘅 agent（代理人）。要問：佢簽約嗰陣係咪喺授權範圍內？仲要識 ostensible authority（表面授權）：就算實際上冇授權，但對外睇落似有授權，第三者信咗，公司都要孭。

🟢 Partnership Property 合夥財產 — s.22 PO

Contributed as capital: money, tools, raw materials, intellectual property.

Miles v Clarke: assets of essential commercial benefit to the partnership.

🔵 中文解說

為咗做生意夾出嚟嘅錢、工具、原材料、知識產權，都係合夥財產。
Miles v Clarke 記呢句：對合夥生意有「重要商業利益」嘅資產，就當係合夥財產，唔係個人財產。

🟢 Advantages

  • Can be formed informally
  • Running depends on agreement
  • Free to make arrangements with creditors

🟢 Disadvantages

  • Liable for torts / breaches of agency by other partners
  • Jointly and severally liable
  • Division between partnership / individual property
  • Terminates on death / bankruptcy of partner

🔵 中文解說：最大伏位

Jointly and severally liable（共同及各別責任） = 債主可以追任何一個拍檔還晒成筆數，唔使逐個追。拍檔做錯嘢（例如侵權、違反代理），其他拍檔都可能要孭。考試見到「拍檔做咗…」即刻諗呢點。

s.7 PO：合夥人行為可以綁住成間合夥。

4 · Company — General 公司總論

Section 04 · Salomon

🟢 Salomon v Salomon

Company is separate & distinct from shareholders, directors and staff.

  • Artificial person
  • Can make contracts
  • Take legal actions; be sued
  • Own property
  • Commit crimes and torts
  • Perpetual succession

🔵 中文解說：考試必寫案例

公司係獨立「人」，唔等於老闆。公司可以自己簽約、告人、俾人告、擁有物業、甚至犯法（犯罪 / 侵權），唔會因為股東死咗就消失。

答題結構：先寫 Salomon → 公司係獨立法人 → 再問邊個要負責（公司定個人）。

🟢 Limited Liability 有限責任

Members’ liability limited to amount uncalled on nominal value of shares issued to them. The company itself has unlimited liability.

🔵 中文解說：最易撈亂

股東只係負責「未繳足嘅股款」。例如股值 $10，已繳 $7，股東最多再俾 $3。但公司本身欠幾多就欠幾多，係無限責任。唔好寫「公司有限責任」！

🟢 Formation Documents 成立文件 — s.15 & s.18(2) CO

  • Memorandum of Association
  • Articles of Association
  • Statement / Declaration of Compliance
  • Shelf companies

🔵 中文解說

成立公司要交：章程大綱 + 章程細則 + 合規聲明。Shelf companies（現成公司） = 已經註冊好、未做過生意嘅空殼公司，買返嚟即刻用得，慳時間。

🟢 Private Company — s.29 CO

Definition of private company. No definition of public company in CO.

🟢 Oversea Companies — s.332 CO, Part XI

Place of business: s.341 CO

🟢 Subsidiaries — s.2 CO

If H controls composition of board, or half of voting rights of S.

🔵 中文解說

私人公司有定義，公開公司喺條例入面冇定義（要靠理解）。海外公司要留意喺香港有營業地點就要登記。母子公司：母公司控制到子公司董事會組成，或者控制一半投票權，就係母子關係。

🟢 Company Advantages / Disadvantages

Adv: Limited liability, perpetual succession, easy finance + floating charge, contract in own name, own property.
Disadv: Disclose info to public, admin expenses, annual audit, sue / be sued, commit torts.

🔵 中文解說

好處 = 有限責任 + 長命 + 借錢易。代價 = 要公開資料、要畀行政費、每年要審計、公司自己都要孭法律責任。

5 · Constitution 公司章程

Section 05

🟢 Main Provisions

Constitution sets out objects. s.5 states info it must contain. Change of name by special resolution. Registered office: s.92.

🔵 中文解說

章程即係公司嘅「憲法」，寫明公司做咩生意（objects）。改名要特別決議（高門檻），註冊辦事處要跟 s.92。

🟢 Objects & Ultra Vires

Objects, ultra vires & constructive notice — abolished. Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 1997: ss.5, 5A, 51.

Objects clause still applies to non-profit organisations.

🔵 中文解說

以前：公司做超出章程範圍嘅事 = ultra vires（越權），可能無效。1997年修例後：呢套已廢除，唔好再用嚟答一般公司。但非牟利機構仍然受 objects clause 限制，考試鍾意考呢個例外。

🟢 Internal Management

  • Memorandum precedes in case of conflict
  • Table A, First Schedule, Model Articles
  • Changes to articles: s.13 by special resolution
  • Power to alter: bona fide for benefit of company

🔵 中文解說

大綱同細則打架 → 大綱贏。改細則要用特別決議，而且要真心為公司利益（bona fide），唔可以為咗欺壓小股東。

6 · Capital & Shares 資本 & 股份

Section 06
英文中文解說記法
Authorised capital
Total nominal amount company is authorised to raise by memorandum		章程話最多可以籌幾多。係「上限」，唔係已經收到嘅錢。授權 = 上限
Issued capital
Nominal value of all shares actually issued		實際發行咗幾多股份嘅面值總和。已發行 = 已派咗
Paid-up capital
Capital gained from shares already issued		股東真係已經俾咗嘅錢。已繳 = 已收錢

🟢 Ordinary Shares 普通股

More risky.

🔵 中文解說

風險較高：派息、清盤分錢都排喺優先股之後。但通常有投票權，控制權較大。

🟢 Preference Shares 優先股

  • Preference depends on articles
  • Usually priority over dividends
  • During liquidation, priority over remaining assets

🔵 中文解說

優先 = 派息優先、分剩餘資產優先。但優先內容要睇章程點寫，唔係自動有。考試要對比普通股嚟答。

7 · Directors, Officers & Governance 董事、職員 & 管治

Section 07

🟢 Fiduciary Duties 受信責任

  1. Duty to act in best interests of company as a whole
  2. Duty to exercise powers for proper purpose
  3. Duty not to fetter discretion
  4. Avoid conflict between duties and interests

s.162 CO · Art.86 — Contracts where director has undeclared interest could be voided.
Also: duty of care and diligence.

🔵 中文解說：董事唔係打工仔，係受託人

① 要為成間公司利益做事，唔係為自己 / 大股東；② 權力要用喺正確目的，唔好濫用；③ 唔好預先綁死自己嘅判斷（例如應承人哋點投票）；④ 唔好自己同公司有利益衝突。

s.162：董事有利益而冇申報，嗰份合約可能被作廢。仲要記：董事仲有謹慎 + 勤勉責任，唔可以求其。

🟢 Secretary & Officers

s.154(1) CO: Every company must have a secretary. Officers include “secretary” (s.2 CO). Extensive duties.

🔵 中文解說

每間公司必須有公司秘書，唔係可有可無。秘書都係 officer，責任好多，唔好以為只係做文書。

🟢 Enforcement & Minority

Foss v Harbottle: Certain matters — incl. alteration of articles & memorandum — require special resolution.

s.168A CO: unfairly prejudicial conduct.

🔵 中文解說

Foss v Harbottle：一般嚟講，告公司嘅權利屬於公司本身，唔係個別股東。但如果大股東欺壓小股東，小股東可以用 s.168A「不公平損害」嚟告。改章程 / 大綱呢啲大事，要特別決議。

🔵 Corporate Governance 公司管治 = 管理層、董事會、股東、持份者之間嘅關係。考試四點：① 向股東問責 + 保護小股東 ② 透明度 ③ 董事責任 + 避免利益衝突 ④ 規管 + 執行。

8 · Winding Up 清盤

Section 08 · 還錢次序最重要

🟢 Types

  • Compulsory — winding up by court
  • Voluntary — a) by members · b) by creditors

Track: 1) Solvency · 2) Appointment of liquidator · 3) Committee of inspection · 4) Meetings

🔵 中文解說

強制清盤 = 法庭頒令；自願清盤 = 股東自願 / 債權人自願。跟住要睇：有冇償債能力 → 委任清盤人 → 監察委員會 → 開會。

🟢 Grounds — s.177(1)

6 grounds for petition. Most popular:

Ground (d) — Inability to pay its debts
Ground (f) — Just and equitable ground

Ebrahimi v Westbourne Galleries: members formed company on basis of mutual trust, understanding & confidence which no longer exists.

🔵 中文解說

6個理由，最常考 (d) 還唔到債 + (f) 公平公正理由。
Ebrahimi 記：原本係基於互信、理解、信心先一齊開公司，而家信任冇晒，法庭可以覺得「公平起見」應該清盤。考試見到「拍檔反面 / 信任破裂」即刻諗呢個案例。

🔴 考試必背 — 還錢次序 s.265(3B) CO（由先到後）：
1. [Fixed charge] 固定押記 → 2. 清盤費用（costs, charges, expenses properly incurred） → 3. 優先債權（preferential debts） → 4. Floating charge 浮動押記 → 5. 無抵押債權（unsecured debts） → 6. 有剩 → 還畀出資人（contributories）

🟢 Fixed Charge 固定押記

Specific charge. Company loses right to deal with assets freely.

🔵 中文解說

押咗邊件資產就係邊件，嗰件資產唔可以隨便賣 / 用，要債主同意。

🟢 Floating Charge 浮動押記

On a certain class of assets. Does not attach to any particular asset, allows company to continue to use them in ordinary course until crystallisation.

Re Spectrum: book debts.

🔵 中文解說

押一類資產（例如全部存貨、應收帳），唔鎖死邊一件，公司日常仲可以用到，直到「結晶」（crystallisation）嗰刻先鎖死。
Re Spectrum 記：應收帳款（book debts）嗰個爭議。
