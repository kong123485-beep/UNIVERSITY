1侵權法分類 — Intentional vs Unintentional

🟠 Intentional Tort 故意侵權

例如：trespass to person、trespass to land & property。

  • 即使 innocent motive（善意動機），亦可構成故意侵權
  • 相反，malicious intent（惡意）未必構成故意侵權
Bradford v Pickles — 惡意本身不等於故意侵權。
Wilkinson v Downton — Intentional 可用 objective test 客觀標準判斷。

🔵 Unintentional Tort 非故意侵權

Tort of negligence & strict liability torts。

  • 不以故意為必要
  • 重點在 duty、breach、causation、damage

Occupiers’ Liability 佔用人責任

s.3 Occupiers Liability Ordinance
(1) An occupier of premises owes the same duty, the “common duty of care”, to all his visitors, except in so far as he is free to and does extend, restrict, modify or exclude his duty to any visitor or visitors by agreement or otherwise.
(2) The common duty of care is a duty to take such care as in all the circumstances of the case is reasonable to see that the visitor will be reasonably safe in using the premises for the purposes for which he is invited or permitted by the occupier to be there.

Trespass to Person

  • Assault 襲擊：the threat / use of force on another that causes that person to have a reasonable apprehension of imminent harmful / offensive contact
  • Battery 毆打：the direct & intentional application of physical force to the person without lawful justification

2Negligence 疏忽 — 四大要素

要成立 negligence，一般須證明：1. Duty of care → 2. Breach（違反 Standard of care）→ 3. Causation → 4. Remoteness / Damage。

要素問題
1. Duty被告是否對原告負有注意義務？
2. Breach被告有否違反應有標準？
3. Causation違反是否造成原告損失？
4. Remoteness損失是否合理可預見？

3Duty of Care 注意義務 — 三大案例演變

Donoghue v Stevenson — Neighbour principle, general duty of care。確立一般注意義務的基礎。
Anns v Merton Borough Council — Two-stage test，但後來受到批評。

現行主流：Caparo 三階段測試

Caparo v Dickman — Three-stage test
i) Reasonable foresight of harm（合理可預見損害）
ii) Sufficient relationship of proximity（足夠親近關係）
iii) Whether it’s fair, just & reasonable（施加義務是否公平、公正及合理）

4Standard of Care · Causation · Remoteness

Standard of Care 注意標準

  • Depends on the circumstances of the case / status of defendant
  • In general, professionals have a higher standard of care
Wharf Properties Ltd case — Complying with the code of conduct, therefore no breach。
Edward Wong Finance Co. v JSM — Standard of care is even higher than the code。
Res ipsa loquitur — the facts speak for themselves（事實自證）
Presumed liability。參考 Ng Chun-pui case 及 s.62 Evidence Ordinance。

3. Causation 因果關係

The breach is the cause of the Plaintiff’s loss。

  • Intervening factors（介入因素）：Yuen Tat-cheong v Urban Council
  • Separate claims if injury caused by more than 1 person：Civil Liability (Contribution) Ordinance

4. Remoteness 遙遠性

Reasonable foreseeability：whether the defendant could reasonably foresee his negligence would cause losses。

Overseas Tankship (UK) Ltd case — If not reasonably foreseeable, defendant is not liable。
Exception：Thin skull rule（蛋殼頭顱規則）
若原告身體特別脆弱，因而比一般人遭受更嚴重傷害，被告仍須負責。

5Defences 抗辯

a) Contributory Negligence 原告共同疏忽

Plaintiff’s duty to minimize the extent of damage。

Au Cheung v Choi Lai-fan
s.21(1) Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance

b) Exemption Clauses 免責條款

  • Death or injury cannot be excluded：s.7 Control of Exemption Clauses Ordinance
  • Other types of losses：reasonable standards（合理標準）

c) Volenti non fit injuria 自願承擔風險

Consent / voluntary assumption of risk — 原告同意或自願承擔風險，被告可作抗辯。

6Professional Negligence & Negligent Misstatement

Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd — Negligent misstatement 過失性失實陳述

要成立，須有：

  • Special relationship 特殊關係
  • Defendant aware of the purpose of advice
  • Advice would be known to plaintiff and be used to fulfil the purpose
  • Defendant knew plaintiff would rely on his advice
  • Plaintiff did rely on the advice & suffered loss as a result
Smith v Bush — Surveyor’s report had been conducted carelessly and therefore, the surveyor was liable to the purchaser in negligence。

Auditors 核數師：Caparo v Dickman

除三階段測試外，另有 2 項進一步準則：

  • v) Auditor could reasonably foresee plaintiff would sustain a loss after relying on advice
  • vi) Fair, just and reasonable to impose duty

重點 Professional negligence：higher standard of care。

7Nuisance 滋擾 & Defamation 誹謗

Private Nuisance 私人滋擾

When landlord or landowner’s interest in a property is disturbed。

Public Nuisance 公共滋擾

When a person in a public place disturbs or harms someone else。

Landlord, owner or occupier of the one who creates the nuisance may also be liable。

Defamation 誹謗

The making of a false statement that harms the plaintiff’s reputation。

  • Slander：oral defamation（口頭誹謗）
  • Libel：written defamation（書面誹謗）

Defences 抗辯

  • Justification 屬實
  • Privilege：absolute and qualified（絕對 / 有限特權）
  • Fair comment：Ma Ching Fat case、Albert Cheng v Tse Wai Chun Paul

8Strict Liability & Vicarious Liability

Strict Liability 嚴格責任

Defendant must compensate despite not being at fault。

Note：Employees Compensation Assistance Ordinance

Vicarious Liability 轉承責任

Defendant is not personally at fault but still liable for others’ wrongs。

e.g. employer for the fault of employee。
