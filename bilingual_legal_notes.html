PART I: HKSAR Legal System / 第一部分：香港特區法律制度

1. Definition & Functions of Law / 法律的定義與功能

EN

A law is a rule of conduct imposed and enforced by the Sovereign. It is the body of principles recognised and applied by the State in the administration of justice.

  • Establishes order and resolves disputes
  • Protects individual rights and property
  • Limits arbitrary state power
中

法律是由主權者（Sovereign）制定並強制執行的行為準則。它是國家在司法行政（administration of justice）中所承認並應用的一整套原則。

  • 維持社會秩序與化解爭議
  • 保障個人權利與私有財產
  • 約束與限制公權力的任意行使

2. Legal Systems & Precedent / 法律體系與判例原則

Common Law System (普通法系)

Based primarily on judicial precedents (case law). Developed by judges through decisions in courts.

主要基於法官在審理案件時建立的司法判例（判例法）。代表地區：香港、英國、澳洲、美國等。

Civil Law System (大陸法系 / 成文法系)

Codified laws. Comprehensive, continuously updated legal codes specify all matters capable of being brought before a court.

以成文法典為核心。代表地區：中國內地、法國、德國、日本等。

Doctrine of Precedent (Stare Decisis) / 遵循前例原則

The ratio decidendi (legal reason for decision) laid down by a superior court is binding on lower courts. Under this doctrine, when material facts of a case are substantially identical to a past precedent, the court is bound to apply that ratio.

Ratio Decidendi 判決理由：案件中具拘束力的法律核心原則。 Binding on lower courts.
Obiter Dicta 附帶意見：法官順帶提及的觀點，無強制約束力但具說服力 (Persuasive).
Equity (衡平法) 公平原則：補充普通法過於僵硬（Rigidity）的不足，確保司法公正。

3. Sources of Law & Basic Law Framework / 香港法律淵源與憲制框架

1. Basic Law (《基本法》)
HKSAR’s constitutional document. Constitutional supremacy. (香港憲制性文件，具最高法律效力)
2. National Laws (全國性法律)
Applied via Annex III under Art. 18. (列於《基本法》附件三並在本地實施的全國性法律)
3. LegCo Statutes (本地立法條例)
Legislation passed by LegCo under Art. 17. (立法會通過的各項本地條例)
4. Common Law & Case Law (判例法)
Precedents from HK and other common law jurisdictions (Art. 84). (香港及其他普通法適用地區的判例)
5. International Treaties (國際條約)
Applied to HK as implemented into domestic legislation. (適用於香港並轉化為本地法的國際公約)
6. Customary Law (習慣法)
Chinese customary law preserved under Art. 8. (主要適用於新界土地相關的中國傳統習慣法)
Article / 條文 English Core Content 中文核心內容
Art. 5, 8, 11 Preserves capitalist system and previous common law system; Basic Law as constitutional standard. 保障資本主義制度及原有的普通法制度不變；《基本法》為最高憲制標準。
Art. 17 LegCo has legislative power to enact laws (except defense & foreign affairs). 立法會享有制定法律的權力（涉及國防及外交等事務除外）。
Art. 158 Power of interpretation of the Basic Law is vested in NPCSC. 《基本法》的最終解釋權屬於全國人民代表大會常務委員會（人大常委會）。
Art. 159 Power of amendment of the Basic Law is vested in the National People's Congress (NPC). 《基本法》的修改權屬於全國人民代表大會（全國人大）。

4. Hong Kong Court Structure & Jurisdiction / 香港法院結構與管轄權

Court of Final Appeal (CFA) / 香港終審法院 Superior / 最高終審機關

Highest appellate court in HKSAR. Headed by the Chief Justice. Hears final civil and criminal appeals.

香港特區最高上訴法院，由終審法院首席法官領導，對刑事及民事上訴擁有最終審判權。

High Court / 香港高等法院 (Superior Court)
Court of Appeal / 上訴法庭 Hears appeals from Court of First Instance and District Court. (負責審理來自原訟法庭及區域法院的上訴)
Court of First Instance / 原訟法庭 Unlimited jurisdiction in civil ($>HKD 3M) & serious criminal cases. (無限制的民事索償審判權，及審理最嚴重的刑事案件)
District Court / 區域法院 (Inferior Court)

Civil jurisdiction: HKD $75,000 to $3,000,000. Criminal jurisdiction: up to 7 years imprisonment.

民事管轄權：$75,000 至 $3,000,000 港幣。刑事監禁上限：最高7年。

Magistrates' Courts / 裁判官法院 (Inferior Court)

Summary criminal offences. Maximum 2 years imprisonment per offence (3 years for multiple).

審理簡易程序刑事罪行。單一罪行最高監禁2年（多項罪行最高3年）。

Specialized Tribunals / 各類特別審裁處
Small Claims: Civil claims ≤ $75,000 (小額錢債)
Labour: Employer-employee disputes (勞資糾紛)
Lands: Tenancy & building management (土地租務)
Obscene Articles: Classification (淫褻物品評級)

PART II: Tort Law Comprehensive Analysis / 第二部分：侵權法深度解析

5. Intentional Torts & Key Legal Motives / 故意侵權與意圖認定

Intentional Torts Principles / 故意侵權原理

  • Innocent Motive: Even an innocent motive may still lead to liability for intentional tort. (無辜或出於善意的動機仍可構成故意侵權)
  • Malicious Intent: Malicious intent alone does not make a lawful act unlawful. (純粹惡意若行為本身合法，不必然構成侵權)
  • Objective Test: Intention can be tested objectively. (意圖可採用客觀標準推算)

Types of Trespass / 侵擾行為分類

  • Assault (恐嚇/襲擊預備): Threat/use of force causing reasonable apprehension of imminent harmful contact.
  • Battery (非法侵犯/毆打): Direct & intentional application of physical force without legal justification.
  • Trespass to Land (侵入土地): Unlawful entry onto another's land.
Bradford v Pickles [1895] AC 587

Rule: Malicious motive does not make a lawful act tortious.

案情與法理：被告在其土地上截留地下水以脅迫市議會高價買地。法院裁定：只要行為人行使其合法權利，即使出於惡意（Malicious Intent），亦不構成侵權。

Wilkinson v Downton [1897] EBH 57

Rule: Intention assessed via Objective Test (Physical/Psychological harm).

案情與法理：被告開惡劣玩笑聲稱原告丈夫遭遇車禍雙腿折斷，致原告精神崩潰。法院裁定：出於客觀檢測，行為人故意做出具高度損害可能性的行為，需對產生的精神損害負責。

6. The Tort of Negligence / 疏忽罪四大成立要素

ELEMENT 1

Duty of Care / 照顧責任

Establishing a legal duty owed by the defendant to the plaintiff to avoid acts/omissions likely to cause harm.

Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] Neighbour Principle (鄰人原則) 必须小心避免可能合理預見會傷害到「鄰人」（即直接受行為影響的人）的舉動。
Anns v Merton [1978] Two-Stage Test (兩步測試) 曾提出的兩步測試，但因過於寬鬆容易引致無限責任而被後續判例批評及修訂。
Caparo v Dickman [1990] Three-Stage Test (三步測試法)
  • i) Reasonable foresight of harm (可合理預見損害)
  • ii) Proximity of relationship (足夠親近關係)
  • iii) Fair, just & reasonable to impose duty (公平、公正及合理)
ELEMENT 2

Standard of Care & Breach / 違反合理照顧標準

The standard is objective: what a reasonable person would do in the circumstances. Professionals are held to a higher standard.

Wharf Properties Ltd Case 被告遵守業界守則（Code of Conduct），因此被裁定已盡合理照顧標準，無違反責任。
Edward Wong Finance v JSM 法院裁定：即使符合行業普遍做法，若該做法本身存在顯而易見的風險，專業標準要求高於守則（Higher standard than the code）。
Res Ipsa Loquitur (事實自明) 事實勝於雄辯：案件事實足以推斷被告疏忽，舉證責任倒置（Presumed liability, e.g. Ng Chun-pui, s.62 Evidence Ordinance）。
ELEMENT 3

Causation / 因果關係

Plaintiff must prove defendant's breach caused the loss ("But for" test).

  • Intervening events (Novus actus interveniens): Yuen Tat-cheong v Urban Council (中斷因果鏈的獨立事件)
  • Multiple Tortfeasors: Civil Liability (Contribution) Ordinance Allows apportionment of liability between joint tortfeasors. (有多名責任人時分擔損害賠償)
ELEMENT 4

Remoteness / 法律距離與損害遙遠性

Losses must be reasonably foreseeable.

  • Overseas Tankship (UK) Ltd (The Wagon Mound No 1): 如果損害種類無法合理預見，被告不承擔責任。
  • Thin Skull Rule (薄頭骨規則/蛋殼頭規則): Exception: Defendant must take the victim as he finds him. (若受害人體質脆弱導致更嚴重傷害，被告仍須承擔全數責任)

7. Professional Negligence & Pure Economic Loss / 專業疏忽與純粹經濟損失

Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd [1964] AC 465

Established liability for negligent misstatement causing pure economic loss under a special relationship.

Special Relationship 4 Criteria / 特殊關係4大測試要素：
  1. Defendant was aware of the specific purpose of the advice. (被告知悉諮詢意見之具體用途)
  2. Advice would be communicated to plaintiff to fulfill that purpose. (該意見會轉交予原告使用)
  3. Defendant knew plaintiff would likely rely on the advice. (被告預計原告會依賴該意見)
  4. Plaintiff did reasonably rely on it and suffered loss. (原告合理依賴並因而承受經濟損失)
Smith v Eric S Bush [1990] 樓宇估值師報告疏忽，估值師明知買家會依賴該報告，故需對買家的損失承擔疏忽責任。
Auditors' Duty: Caparo v Dickman 審計師對潛在收購者/一般投資者不負有直接照顧責任，除非符合可合理預見及 Fair, Just & Reasonable 標準。

8. Occupiers' Liability & Nuisance / 佔用人責任與滋擾

Occupiers Liability Ordinance (Cap. 314) s.3 / 《佔用人責任條例》第3條

s.3(1) Common Duty of Care An occupier of premises owes the same duty, the "common duty of care", to all his visitors (except where extended, modified, or excluded by agreement). （佔用人對所有訪客負有相同的「一般照顧責任」）
s.3(2) Reasonable Safety Standard Take such care as in all the circumstances is reasonable to see that the visitor will be reasonably safe in using the premises for permitted purposes. （在所有合理情況下確保訪客按許可用途使用場所時人身安全）
Private Nuisance / 私人滋擾

Unreasonable interference with a person's use or enjoyment of land/property.

不合理地干擾他人對土地/物業的使用或享用權。物業業主或實際干擾製造者需承擔責任。

Public Nuisance / 公眾滋擾

An act materially affecting the reasonable comfort and convenience of life of a class of the public.

嚴重影響不特定廣大公眾安全、健康或生活便利的行為。

9. Defamation Law & Defences / 誹謗法及其法定抗辯

Libel vs Slander / 誹謗分類

Libel (書面/永久形式誹謗): Defamatory statement in permanent form (written text, published articles, broadcasts). Actionable per se.
Slander (口頭/暫時形式誹謗): Defamatory statement in transient form (spoken words, gestures). Generally requires proof of special damage.

Defences to Defamation / 誹謗法的免責抗辯

  • Justification (真實理由): Statement is true in substance and in fact. (陳述內容屬實)
  • Absolute Privilege (絕對特權): Statements in court or LegCo proceedings. (立法會或法庭審判發言)
  • Qualified Privilege (受條件限制的特權): Legal, moral or social duty to communicate. (履行法律或道德責任之傳達)
  • Fair Comment / Honest Opinion (公正評論): Ma Ching Fat case; Albert Cheng v Tse Wai Chun Paul (基於事實的公眾利益事項公正評論)
Special Remedy in Defamation (誹謗特別救濟): Published Apology (刊登道歉聲明, e.g., Chu Siu Kuk Yuen v Apple Daily Ltd).

10. Strict Liability, Vicarious Liability & Remedies / 嚴格責任、代位責任與法律救濟

Strict Liability (無過失/嚴格責任)

Defendant liable without proof of fault or negligence.

無需證明過失即須賠償（例如：Employees Compensation Assistance Ordinance 僱員補償援助條例）。

Vicarious Liability (代位責任)

Employer liable for torts committed by employee in course of employment.

僱主需為僱員在執行職務期間（Course of Employment）造成的侵權行為承擔代位法律責任。

Injunctions (禁制令)

Equitable remedy ordering party to do or refrain from doing an act.

由法院發出的公平法救濟命令，強制停止或執行特定行為。

Types of Damages / 損害賠償種類

  • General damages (一般賠償): Non-quantifiable losses (Pain & suffering). (痛苦與失能)
  • Special damages (特別賠償): Out-of-pocket financial losses up to trial. (可核算之財務損失)
  • Nominal damages (象徵性賠償): Right violated but no actual financial loss.
  • Aggravated damages (加重性賠償): Compensate for extra injury to feelings.
  • Exemplary damages (懲罰性賠償): Punish outrageous conduct by defendant.

Limitation Ordinance (Cap. 347) / 《時效條例》

Action Type / 訴訟類別 Section Time Limit
Personal Injury (Negligence/Nuisance) / 人身傷害 S.27 3 Years (3年)
General Torts & Contract / 一般侵權與合約 S.4(1) 6 Years (6年)
